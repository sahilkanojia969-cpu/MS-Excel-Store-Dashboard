📊 Vrinda Store Annual Sales Report 2022 – Excel Dashboard Analysis
________________________________________
📌 1. Executive Summary
This project analyses the annual sales performance of Vrinda Store (2022) using Excel-based data modelling and dashboard visualization.
The objective was to identify:
•	Sales trends across months
•	Gender-wise revenue contribution
•	Top-performing states
•	Channel-wise sales distribution
•	Order fulfilment performance
•	Age-group purchasing behaviour
The dashboard provides decision-ready insights for business growth and operational optimization.
________________________________________
🎯 2. Business Problem Statement
Vrinda Store wanted to:
•	Understand revenue drivers in 2022
•	Identify high-performing states and sales channels
•	Analyse customer demographics
•	Evaluate order fulfilment efficiency
•	Detect opportunities to improve revenue and reduce cancellations
________________________________________
📂 3. Dataset Overview
The dataset includes:
•	Order ID
•	Order Date (Month-wise)
•	Gender
•	Age
•	State
•	Sales Channel
•	Category
•	Order Status
•	Sales Amount

Data was cleaned and transformed using:
•	Pivot Tables
•	Data Validation
•	Filtering & Slicers
•	KPI aggregation
________________________________________
📈 4. Key KPIs Defined
KPI	Description
Total Sales	Sum of all order amounts
Total Orders	Count of Order IDs
Order Fulfilment Rate	Delivered Orders %
Gender Contribution %	Revenue split by Men vs Women
Channel Contribution %	Revenue by platform
Top States by Revenue	Highest performing regions
Age Segment Distribution	Revenue by demographic segment
________________________________________
🔎 5. Key Insights from Dashboard
________________________________________
🟠 5.1 Sales Trend (Order vs Sales)
•	Sales peaked between January–March
•	Gradual decline observed after August
•	Correlation visible between order count and revenue
•	Q1 generated highest revenue contribution
📌 Insight: Business performs strongest in early financial year.
________________________________________
👩‍🦰 5.2 Gender-Based Sales
•	Women contributed 64% of total sales
•	Men contributed 36%
📌 Insight: Women are primary revenue drivers.
Marketing efforts should prioritize female customer base.
________________________________________
📦 5.3 Order Status Performance
•	92% Orders Delivered
•	3% Cancelled
•	3% Returned
•	2% Refunded
📌 Insight:
•	Strong operational efficiency
•	Low cancellation & refund rate
•	Healthy fulfilment system
________________________________________
🏆 5.4 Top 5 States by Sales
1.	Maharashtra – 2.99M
2.	Karnataka – 2.64M
3.	Uttar Pradesh – 2.10M
4.	Telangana – 1.71M
5.	Tamil Nadu – 1.67M
📌 Insight:
Western & Southern states dominate revenue.
Recommendation:
Increase targeted campaigns in Maharashtra & Karnataka.
________________________________________
👥 5.5 Age & Gender Analysis
Adult Women (34.59%) represent the highest buying segment.
Segment Contribution:
•	Adult Women – Highest
•	Teenage Women – Strong segment
•	Senior segment – Lower revenue contribution
📌 Insight:
Core customer persona = Adult Female shoppers.
________________________________________
🛒 5.6 Channel Performance
•	Amazon – 35% (Highest)
•	Myntra – 23%
•	Flipkart – 22%
•	Ajio – 6%
•	Meesho – 5%
•	Nalli – 5%
•	Others – 4%
📌 Insight:
Marketplace dependency is high on Amazon.
Risk:
Over-reliance on a single channel.
Recommendation:
Strengthen Flipkart & Myntra marketing.
________________________________________
🧠 6. Business Recommendations
1.	Increase marketing budget toward women-focused campaigns
2.	Strengthen Q4 promotional activities to counter sales decline
3.	Diversify revenue channels to reduce Amazon dependency
4.	Expand inventory in Maharashtra & Karnataka
5.	Introduce loyalty programs for Adult Female customers
